# Feedback

Thanks for reading *Programming the Loop: What I Measured with Coding Agents*.

If something helped, confused you, or felt off, I'd like to hear it. Honest criticism is the most useful kind — vague praise is nice, but a specific "this bit didn't land" is what actually improves the next draft.

## Two ways to write in

- **[Book feedback](https://github.com/meabs/ProgrammingTheLoop-WorkedExample/issues/new?template=book-feedback.yml)** — what worked, what was unclear, and anything you felt was missing.
- **[Correction or error](https://github.com/meabs/ProgrammingTheLoop-WorkedExample/issues/new?template=correction.yml)** — a wrong fact, a typo, or something that misleads. Please say where it is and what it ought to say.

You can also open a blank issue if neither form fits.

## Corrections and credit

If you send a correction and you'd like to be named, say so in the issue. Corrections will be credited in the next edition when you want that.

No need to dress it up. Short notes are fine.
